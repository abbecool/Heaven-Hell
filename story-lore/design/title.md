# Title Direction

**Status: Working project decision.**

The current working title is **Forger: The Hollow King**.

*Heaven and Hell* is the former title. Earlier title exploration is retained in `archive/` and must not be treated as the current title decision.

The working title supports the player’s initially mistaken connection to the Forge Kingdom, the hollowed and fragmented state of Gudloth, and the false claim to kingship that the story later overturns.
