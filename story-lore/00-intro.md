# Heaven and Hell: Intro

In a world still scarred by the fall of the Forge Kingdom and the Six Day Battle, a weak, ancient spirit awakens on an old battlefield. Believing itself to be the remnant of a defeated ruler, it must cross a land divided between hungry city knights, nature-bound archers, newly arrived dwarves, and wizards guarding a rightful king in the northeast.

The player survives by draining weaker creatures and possessing living hosts. Each body provides its own weapons, abilities, identity, and routes through the world, so progression means experiencing the conflict from within several opposing factions. As the spirit grows stronger and reaches the old city, buried history and its own forgotten identity begin to challenge everything it believes about its mission.
