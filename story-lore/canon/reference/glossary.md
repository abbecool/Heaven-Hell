# Glossary

**Status: Canon reference.**

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| The Visit | Unknown event that begins the calendar in Year 0. |
| Forge Kingdom | Ancient regional civilisation destroyed by the Six Day Battle. |
| Gudloth | Former wizard and demigod-like enemy of the Forge Kingdom; secretly the returned player character. |
| Six Day Battle | Six-day conflict in Year 903 between the Forge Kingdom and Gudloth's followers on the rock plains. |
| La Poise | Modern human successor city on the Lace River. |
| Old City | Later city reinhabited among the northern Forge ruins. |
| Enchanted Lands | Gudloth's homeland and a historical source of scattered mages. |
| Old Woods | Forest where Gudloth developed his power. |
| Rock Plains | Ancient battlefield of the Six Day Battle. |
| Cursed Sea, Rocky Cliffs, Forge Barrier | Working or legendary place names. Their final locations, survival, or nature are not established. |
