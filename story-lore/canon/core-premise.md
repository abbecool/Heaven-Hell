# Core Premise

**Status: Canon overview.** This is a short orientation document; the linked subject files are the detailed sources of truth.

In Year 1720 After the Visit, a weak demon or spirit awakens on the ancient battlefield of the Six Day Battle. It survives by draining life and possessing living people. Each host gives the player abilities, social identity, and a perspective on a world that continued for 817 years without Gudloth.

The player is Gudloth returned in a diminished form, but neither the player nor the modern world knows this at the beginning. The entity can plausibly seem to be a lost savior, hero, king, or wronged being while evidence increasingly suggests that Gudloth has returned.

The modern conflicts predate the awakening. La Poise needs land and resources for its growing population; elves defend forests and territory; a newly arrived dwarf expedition investigates the loss of earlier local dwarves; and wizards preserve dangerous fragments of the past. Their perspectives are all necessary to reach the Old City and uncover the history of the Forge Kingdom.

The Old City's king is the legitimate descendant of the last Forge king and Gudloth's eventual opposing heir. The king's knowledge of the past and the wizards' relationship to the city are deliberately unresolved. The late revelation is that the player is Gudloth, not the rightful Forge heir they may have believed themself to be.
