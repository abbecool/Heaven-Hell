# Chapter One: Awakening

**Status: Canon opening sequence.**

The player, a weak demon or spirit, awakens on the ancient battlefield of the Six Day Battle. The only path forward is a small passage guarded by a hostile knight. The knight does not recognize the player; it sees only an unknown creature emerging from a dangerous ruin.

The player is too weak to use possession effectively against the guard. A chicken nearby can be drained to restore enough strength to continue. How the player gets past the knight remains open for gameplay design; this encounter should not make the knight the player's first lasting host.

## Story Quest One

Drain the chicken to regain strength.

## Story Quest Two

Get past the battlefield guard and leave the ancient battlefield.

Outside, the player reaches a busy merchant road. A wizard, elf, dwarf, and knight are present as potential hosts. The player must choose and possess one of them to progress; this is the first major faction choice.

## Story Quest Three

Possess one of the four potential hosts.
