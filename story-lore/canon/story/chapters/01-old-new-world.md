# Chapter 1: The dwarfen old new world

## Story
The dwarfs have a settlement on the coastline by the northwestern mountains where they have been for a number of years. They know that dwarfs left their homeland long ago but do not know where they went. The Dwarfs were an integral part of the forge kingdoms long ago. They will eventually learn this and discover old ruins and artifacts.

The dwarfs questline begin when the player first posses a dwarf. Depending of how far along the questlines of other factions has progressed that dwarfen questline might be shorter.

---
## Quest 1: Return back to the settlement from the city with the gold and traded items.
The possessed dwarf is carrying a bag or draging a cart from the city. Maybe he is sleeping by a boulder, tired from the journey so far. The dwarf awakens, and is possessed. He continues the journey to the settlement. The players continues the dwarfs journey.

---
## Quest 2: Protect the treasure.
On the way back the dwarf meets both monsters (rooters and goblins) and bandits. He most defend the treasure by defeating the enemies.

---
## Quest 3: Finally home.
When arriving home he is greated by the other dwarfs. They ask him about the city and the dangerouse journey back. 

Now the player is free to explore, do sidequests, continue the main story or even find another faction to possess to switch questlines.
