# Chapter 1: Life in the city

## Story
The knight has captured an elder elf and must return with the prisoner to the city for interegations. 

The knights questline begin when the player first posses a knight. Depending on how far along the questlines of other factions has progressed the knight questline might be shorter.

---
## Quest 1: Protect the prisoner
The knight escorts the prisoner from the country side/merchant road back to the city La Poise.

---
## Quest 2: Defend against the attack
Either the elfs or some bandits attack the knight to free the prisoner. Defeat all enemies to progress. 

---
## Quest 3: Return home
Complete the journey home and deliver the prisoner to the military/prison.

Now the player is free to explore, do sidequests, continue the main story or even find another faction to possess to switch questlines.
