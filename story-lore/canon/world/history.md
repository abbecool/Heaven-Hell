## The Forge Kingdom

For centuries, the Forge Kingdom was the region's largest city and a dominant political and economic centre. Its trade, industry, protection, and prosperity supported smaller settlements. Humans, dwarves, elves, and organised mages lived and fought within the same broader civilisation.

Elves retained forest settlements while participating in the kingdom's economy. Dwarves served as soldiers, blacksmiths, and craftsmen; mountain communities supplied ore. Wizards were fewer in number and worked as scholars, scientists, librarians, and researchers, including researchers of poorly understood ancient magic. This former coexistence contrasts with the divided modern factions.

## The Six Day Battle and Its Aftermath

The battle lasted six days and devastated the Forge Kingdom despite its immense forces. It occurred during a dark-magic peak and near a nature-magic trough, giving Gudloth a decisive advantage. Gudloth was defeated, destroyed, sealed, or otherwise removed from the world; the exact event remains unknown.

Its established consequences are:

- The Forge Kingdom ceased to be a major political power.
- The local dwarven population was completely wiped out.
- Elven survivors withdrew from the fallen kingdom.
- Surviving mages scattered or retreated toward wizard regions, including the Enchanted Lands.
- Human survivors dispersed into smaller settlements.
- Much of the old civilisation became ruins, burial grounds, or reused material.

The northern city lay abandoned for a long time and was later reinhabited as the [Old City](../factions/old-city.md). It is not a continuous Forge state or population. A surviving Forge Barrier or hidden Forge state is not established.

## Modern Era

[La Poise](../factions/la-poise-and-knights.md) developed from post-Forge settlements around the Lace River and fertile land. It is a human successor civilisation, not Gudloth's city or empire.

The modern dwarves are a recent expedition from a distant civilisation, probably arriving from the west by sea and founding a mountain settlement. Their discoveries of old mines, graves, inscriptions, weapons, and ruins begin to reveal the fate of earlier local dwarves.

The objective sequence is settled; why Gudloth fought, what left him diminished, and what the Old City's king knows remain open questions.
