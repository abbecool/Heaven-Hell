# Magic Cycles

**Status: Canon.** The causes and full consequences of the cycles remain unresolved.

## The Two Forces

- **Dark ancient magic** is Gudloth's primary force and peaks every **903 years**.
- **Nature or life force** peaks every **40 years**. Its lowest phase occurs roughly 20 years after each peak.

The periods are coprime, so both forces peak together only every **36,120 years**.

## Known Cycle Points

| Year After the Visit | Event | Dark ancient magic | Nature/life force |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0 | The Visit | Peak | Peak |
| 903 | Six Day Battle | Peak | Almost at its trough |
| 1720 | Start of the game; Gudloth returns | Strong, 86 years before its next peak | Peak |
| 1806 | Next dark-magic peak | Peak | Early in a new nature cycle |

At Year 903, the nature cycle is 23 years from its peak and therefore just beyond its approximate trough. At Year 1720, life force is at a full peak. The precise shape of the dark-magic cycle between peaks is not yet defined; “strong” describes its story role at the game's opening, not a numerical rule.

Gudloth's return is enabled by the Year 1720 combination of peak life force and strong dark ancient magic. The Six Day Battle had the opposite imbalance: dark magic at its maximum while nature force was almost absent.
