# Gudloth

**Status: Canon.**

Gudloth was an exceptionally powerful wizard from the Enchanted Lands. He rejected its traditions and spent decades in the Old Woods studying and developing magic, including the poorly understood ancient magic investigated by earlier scholars. He became effectively a demigod, gathered followers and powerful apprentices, and eventually came into conflict with the Forge Kingdom.

Gudloth is the player character returned in Year 1720 as a weak demon or spirit-like entity. This is a late-story revelation, not knowledge given to the player or modern world at the beginning. The returned entity may instead appear to be a lost savior, hero, king, or wronged being.

Gudloth's motives, the form of his defeat or sealing in the Six Day Battle, and his immediate ultimate goal remain unresolved. A known active modern cult or army serving him is not established. Ancient apprentices and their traces can inform ruins, relics, records, and boss design without creating such an order.
