# Elves

**Status: Canon.**

Elves survived the fall of the Forge Kingdom and retained cultural and oral memories of the ancient world. Those traditions may preserve genuine information, but centuries of retelling can also distort them.

In the modern era, elves oppose La Poise's expansion into forests and territory they consider theirs. They are nature-connected archers who can be nomadic, stealthy, and brutally effective in defence of the land. Their conflict with La Poise is morally ambiguous: neither survival through urban expansion nor defence of forest communities makes a side wholly right or wrong.

An elf host grants mobility, stealth, forest traversal, and access to hidden natural routes. The elves' preferred name, social structure, and final regional habitat remain open questions.
