# La Poise and the Knights

**Status: Canon.**

La Poise is a modern human city that developed around the Lace River after the Forge Kingdom's collapse. It is a successor civilisation, not Gudloth's foundation or a continuation of his empire.

The knights protect the city, its roads, farmers, settlements, and territorial expansion. Population growth has increased La Poise's need for food, farmland, resources, and territory. Its expansion into forests and fertile land creates a real conflict with the elves: the city faces scarcity, while the elves see their homes and forests being destroyed.

The knights do not serve Gudloth, do not await his return, and do not know the opening demon's identity. A knight host provides heavy combat, defence, authority, and access to human-controlled spaces.
