# Dwarf Expedition

**Status: Canon.**

The dwarves who lived in the Forge Kingdom were wiped out in the Six Day Battle. The current mountain settlement is a recent expedition from another dwarven civilisation, probably arriving from the west by sea. It is not a continuous local dwarf population.

Because no dwarves had lived locally for centuries, many people believed them extinct, fictional, or folklore. The newcomers mine, trade, and explore while discovering old mines, graves, weapons, inscriptions, and ruins made by the people who came before them. Their language and culture may differ enough that ancient inscriptions are recognisable but difficult to read.

A dwarf host provides mining, obstacle breaking, underground access, metalwork, and durability. The expedition's central historical question is what happened to the earlier local dwarves.
