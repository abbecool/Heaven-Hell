# Wizards

**Status: Canon.**

Wizards preserve the strongest surviving written records of the ancient world. Their predecessors lived in the Forge Kingdom in small numbers as scholars, scientists, librarians, and students of nature and poorly understood ancient magic. Gudloth later studied and mastered that same poorly understood magic.

After the kingdom's fall, surviving mages scattered or retreated toward wizard regions and continued incomplete research. Some groups may know Gudloth was real and that the Six Day Battle concealed something important, but no group is established as knowing the full truth.

Wizards can be keepers of archives, magic, seals, and supernatural routes. A wizard host grants magical knowledge and access through barriers and seals. Their relationship to the Old City and its king is deliberately unresolved: do not present them as confirmed royal loyalists, guardians, or a unified enemy of the player.
