# The Forge Kingdom

**Status: Canon.** See [World History](../world/history.md) for its chronology and fall.

The Forge Kingdom was the ancient regional civilisation destroyed in the Six Day Battle. Its wealth and influence came from trade, industry, protection, and cooperation between humans, dwarves, elves, and organised mages.

The kingdom's mixed population is important context, not proof that its modern successor cultures should agree. Elves maintained forest settlements while relying in part on the kingdom's economy and urban elf population. Dwarves worked as soldiers, blacksmiths, and craftsmen, while mountain communities supplied ore. Wizards served as scholars, scientists, librarians, and researchers of nature and ancient magic.

Its surviving evidence—battlefield remains, ruins, mines, graves, weapons, and inscriptions—is valuable precisely because different factions interpret it differently. The former northern city was abandoned and later reinhabited as the [Old City](old-city.md); it is not a surviving Forge government.
