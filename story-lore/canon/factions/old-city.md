# The Old City and Its King

**Status: Canon.**

The Old City occupies the northern ruins of the Forge Kingdom. It was abandoned after the Six Day Battle and reinhabited much later by a new population; it is not a continuously surviving Forge state or population.

Living among the ruins gives the city access to ancient structures, records, and artefacts unavailable elsewhere. Its current king rules the later city and is the legitimate descendant of the last Forge king. The exact route by which that lineage survived and became recognised is not yet detailed.

For most people, the king is a distant and mysterious ruler, not an openly evil enemy. The king's knowledge of Gudloth, the Six Day Battle, and the old kingdom is unresolved. So is the relationship between the city and the wizards.
