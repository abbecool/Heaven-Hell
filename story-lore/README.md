# Heaven and Hell Story Lore

This folder is the working lore reference for *Heaven and Hell*. The current canon is consolidated in [Revised World History and Story Structure](Revised%20World%20History%20and%20Story%20Structure.md). The remaining files expand specific subjects and must agree with that document.

## Canon Status

The revised history combines the useful parts of the original lore with the newer possession-and-faction story. It establishes the following foundations:

- The Forge Kingdom was an ancient multi-people civilization.
- Gudloth rose against it, and the Six Day Battle destroyed the kingdom in 903 After the Visit.
- The game begins in Year 1720, 817 years after that battle. Gudloth disappeared, the local dwarves died out, and new societies developed independently.
- Dark magic peaks every 903 years and nature magic every 40 years. Both peaked at the Visit; the battle occurred during a dark-magic peak and near a nature-magic trough.
- The Old City is the northern remnant of the Forge Kingdom, abandoned after the battle and reinhabited much later as a new city among the ruins.
- La Poise is a modern human successor city on the Lace River; Gudloth did not found it and its knights do not serve the demon.
- A recently arrived dwarf expedition has founded the local mountain settlement and is rediscovering the lost history of its predecessors.
- The player awakens as a weak demon or spirit on the ancient battlefield, then first possesses a dwarf, elf, wizard, or knight on the merchant road.
- The four faction arcs are perspectives on one progressing story, not four isolated campaigns.

The exact nature of The Visit, Gudloth's defeat, and the current king's knowledge of the Old City deliberately remains unresolved. Gudloth's identity is established: the player is Gudloth returned in a weakened demon or spirit-like form. His return is enabled by the Year 1720 peak of nature/life force alongside strong dark ancient magic. Do not present speculative answers as established lore.

## Core Premise

The player is a weak demon or spirit awakening in Year 1720, in a world that has continued for 817 years without it. To survive and grow, it drains life and possesses real people with existing lives, relationships, and responsibilities. Each host gives access to a faction perspective and abilities needed to uncover contradictory fragments of the ancient history.

Modern La Poise expands into forests and fertile territory because its growing population needs food and resources. Elves resist that expansion. Dwarves, knights, elves, and wizards each carry a different partial understanding of the past. The player eventually gains access to the Old City and confronts its king, the rightful descendant of the Forge crown.

The central twist is hidden at the start: the player can seem to be a lost savior, hero, or king, while evidence suggests Gudloth has returned. The Old City's king is initially a distant mystery to investigate, not an openly evil ruler. The later revelation is that the player is Gudloth, the ancient villain, and the king is his legitimate opposing heir.
